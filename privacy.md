# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 24, 2026**

SpecDevShip ("SpecDevShip," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or purchase our products, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Information you provide directly:**

- **Email address** — when you sign up for the free Field Guide or otherwise join our mailing list.
- **Name** (optional) — if you provide it when signing up or at checkout.
- **Payment information** — when you purchase the Skill Pack. Payment details (card numbers, etc.) are collected and processed directly by our third-party payment processor; we do not receive or store your full payment card information ourselves.
- **Contact form messages** — your name, email address, and message when you use the contact form on our website.
- **Anything you send us directly** — for example, if you email us with a support request or refund request.
- **A record of your signup** — when you join our mailing list, we keep the date and time, the form you used, and the wording you agreed to, as a record of your consent.

**Information collected automatically:**

- **Basic usage and analytics data** — such as pages visited, referring source, device/browser type, and approximate location (derived from IP address), collected via standard website analytics tools.
- **Bot-protection signals** — when you use our contact form, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the request comes from a person rather than an automated program. To do this, Cloudflare processes information about your browser and device, including your IP address.
- **Cookies and similar technologies** — small files stored in your browser to support basic site functionality and to understand aggregate usage of our site. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect some site functionality.

We do not knowingly collect any special categories of sensitive personal data (such as health, financial account, or biometric data) beyond what's needed to process a standard payment transaction, which is handled by our payment processor.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Deliver the products you've requested or purchased (for example, emailing you the Field Guide or your Skill Pack download/access link);
- Send you the email sequence associated with signing up for the Field Guide, including occasional promotional messages about the Skill Pack and other SpecDevShip updates;
- Respond to support requests, refund requests, contact form messages, and other communications you send us;
- Keep a record of your consent to receive our emails;
- Process payments and maintain records required for accounting, tax, and legal compliance;
- Understand, in aggregate, how our website and emails are performing, so we can improve them;
- Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, spam, or security issues, including protecting our forms from automated submissions.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## 3. Email Communications

If you sign up for the Field Guide or otherwise join our list, you'll receive a series of emails, including product information and occasional promotional offers, via our email service provider. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and you may opt out of marketing emails at any time. Opting out of marketing emails does not affect transactional emails necessary to deliver a product you've purchased (for example, a purchase receipt or delivery link).

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on third-party service providers to operate our business, including:

- **Email marketing platform** (currently Kit, formerly ConvertKit) — to manage our mailing list and send emails;
- **Transactional email provider** (currently Resend) — to deliver messages sent through our contact form to us;
- **Database provider** (currently Neon) — to store our mailing list records and contact form messages;
- **Website hosting** (currently Vercel) — to host our website and run the services behind its forms;
- **Bot protection** (currently Cloudflare Turnstile) — to protect our contact form from spam and automated abuse;
- **Payment processor** — to securely process purchases of the Skill Pack;
- **Analytics providers** — to understand aggregate traffic to our site.

Each of these providers processes your information under their own privacy policies and security practices. We choose providers that maintain reasonable data protection standards, but we encourage you to review their respective privacy policies as well.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our products and services, comply with our legal obligations (such as tax and accounting records), resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. If you unsubscribe from our mailing list, we retain a minimal record of your unsubscribe status to ensure we don't email you again, along with other data we're legally required to keep (e.g., purchase and payment records). We keep contact form messages for as long as needed to respond to you and handle any follow-up.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable data protection law (such as the GDPR in the European Economic Area/UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), including the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you;
- Correct inaccurate information;
- Request deletion of your information, subject to our legitimate need to retain certain records (e.g., for tax or legal compliance);
- Object to or restrict certain processing;
- Withdraw consent for marketing emails at any time (via the unsubscribe link or by contacting us directly);
- Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our products and website are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process and store information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required by applicable law, we take steps intended to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection in the jurisdictions in which we process it.
